the artist behind the magic

Hi, I'm Summer — creator of sumthing cute!

I'm a 14-year-old artist who fell in love with polymer clay sculpting and turned that passion into a real business. Every single figure I make is sculpted entirely by hand — no molds, no machines, just my hands, my tools, and a lot of creativity.

I make characters, cute animals, superhero mashups, pencil toppers, magnets and more — each one a tiny little world of its own. You can find my work at local art festivals and farmers markets, or order something custom just for you!
